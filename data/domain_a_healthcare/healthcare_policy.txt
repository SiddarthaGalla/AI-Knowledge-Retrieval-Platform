HEALTHCARE & MEDICAL OPERATIONAL GUIDELINES (POLICY 2026-HC)
====================================================================

SECTION 1: EMPLOYEE MEDICAL COVERAGE & ELIGIBILITY
Employees become eligible for full comprehensive medical coverage after completing 30 days of continuous employment with the company. Prior to 30 days, emergency medical stabilization is covered up to $2,000 per incident. Full coverage includes primary care, specialist visits, inpatient hospitalization, and generic prescription medication with a standard $15 copay per prescription.

SECTION 2: OUT-OF-NETWORK MEDICAL CLAIM REIMBURSEMENT
To submit an out-of-network medical claim for reimbursement, employees must follow these exact procedural steps:
1. Obtain an itemized medical bill containing standard CPT procedure codes and ICD-10 diagnosis codes from the attending provider.
2. Complete Form HC-104 (Out-of-Network Claim Submission Form) available on the employee portal within 90 calendar days of service.
3. Attach original payment receipts or proof of payment.
4. Submit the completed claim packet via the portal or via registered mail to the Claims Department. Reimbursements are processed within 14 business days of verified receipt.

SECTION 3: PRE-AUTHORIZATION REQUIREMENTS
Specialized elective surgical procedures, MRI scans, CT scans, and inpatient hospital admissions require pre-authorization from the Health Review Board at least 5 business days prior to scheduled treatment. Emergency admissions must be reported within 48 hours of admission. Failure to secure pre-authorization results in a 30% reduction in eligible reimbursement coverage.

SECTION 4: COMPARATIVE COVERAGE PLAN A VS PLAN B
Plan A (Standard PPO Plan) features a $500 annual individual deductible, $1,500 family deductible, 80/20 coinsurance split after deductible, and an out-of-pocket maximum of $3,500.
Plan B (High-Deductible Health Plan with HSA) features a $2,000 annual individual deductible, $4,000 family deductible, 90/10 coinsurance split after deductible, and an out-of-pocket maximum of $4,500. Plan B includes a company HSA contribution of $1,000 per calendar year.

SECTION 5: DENTAL & VISION SUPPLEMENTAL COVERAGE
Dental coverage includes two preventative cleanings per year at 100% coverage with zero deductible. Major restorative dental services are covered at 50% up to an annual maximum benefit of $2,000 per individual. Vision coverage provides a $200 annual allowance for frames or contact lenses with an annual comprehensive eye exam covered in full.
